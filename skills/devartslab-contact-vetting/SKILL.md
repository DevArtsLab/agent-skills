# Contact Vetting

## Interaction record

Log each inbound contact with:

- Timestamp and date of communication
- Contact: name, title, organization, staffing firm or recruiter representation
- Channel: LinkedIn, direct email, phone, other
- Core subject: role title, project description, inquiry topic
- Governance notes: corporate structure, compliance posture, end client
- Action taken: archived, declined, scheduled call, proceeded

## Vetting criteria

Evaluate the institution, not the pitch:

- Corporate structure: founder-led venture-backed vs publicly traded with board
- Regulatory exposure, when federal or defense-adjacent:
  - FOCI: DCSA review of ownership, control, influence
  - FASCSA: supply chain risk and vendor pedigree in federal procurement
  - CFIUS: foreign stakes in technology or critical infrastructure
- Tech stack and end client: trace the platform, agency, or prime contractor
  behind the role; check staffing-firm pedigree when third-party

## SOP

1. Extract and date: sender, timestamp, channel, message substance.
2. Contextualize: prime contractor, parent org, platform or agency served.
3. Audit governance against the vetting criteria above.
4. Decide: proceed, decline, or archive. Record the action and reasoning.
